# 🛡️ TrustScan

### AI-Powered Scam, Phishing & Fraud Detection Platform

TrustScan is an intelligent cybersecurity platform that helps users identify and avoid online scams, phishing attacks, fake websites, malicious QR codes, and fraudulent messages in real time.

Powered by Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, TrustScan analyzes suspicious content and provides instant risk assessments, helping users stay safe in today's digital world.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🔍 AI Text Scam Detection

Analyze suspicious emails, SMS messages, social media messages, and chat conversations for signs of fraud and phishing.

### 🌐 URL & Website Verification

Detect fake websites, malicious links, phishing pages, and suspicious domains before visiting them.

### 📷 Screenshot Analysis

Upload screenshots of messages, websites, or advertisements and let AI identify potential scam indicators.

### 📱 QR Code Security Scanner

Scan QR codes and verify whether they redirect to safe or potentially dangerous destinations.

### 🎥 Camera-Based Scanning

Use your device camera to scan URLs, QR codes, messages, or suspicious content instantly.

### 🤖 Machine Learning Risk Assessment

Advanced AI models analyze patterns commonly associated with:

* Phishing attacks
* Investment scams
* Giveaway scams
* Impersonation fraud
* Cryptocurrency scams
* Fake job offers
* Social engineering attacks

### ⚡ Real-Time Analysis

Receive instant results with risk scores and security recommendations.

### 🔐 User Authentication

Secure account creation and login system with personalized scan history.

### 🎁 Free Trial Scans

New users receive free scans before upgrading to premium protection plans.

---

## 🧠 How TrustScan Works

TrustScan combines Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing (NLP), Optical Character Recognition (OCR), and threat intelligence techniques to evaluate digital content.

### Analysis Workflow

1. User submits text, URL, QR code, image, or screenshot.
2. AI extracts and analyzes the content.
3. Threat indicators are identified.
4. Risk score is generated.
5. User receives an easy-to-understand security report.
